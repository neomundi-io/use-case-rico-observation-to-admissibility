# Case 02 — Semantic Drift

## What was observed?

A runtime condition exhibiting semantic drift was observed.

Measurement status was complete, but stability and coherence were materially reduced compared with the prior observed state. Elevated semantic variability and high interpretive risk were detected.

Observed continuity indicates suspected discontinuity with material variation from the previous observation.

---

## What evidence exists?

Evidence exists as runtime signal only.

The observation identifies:

- uncertain factual signal;
- elevated semantic instability;
- high semantic drift;
- interpretive variability;
- measurable change from the prior observed condition.

The payload explicitly states that the observation identifies a changed condition but does not determine which interpretation is correct.

---

## What authority exists?

No execution authority is established by the observation.

NeoMundi provides an observed runtime condition only.

The payload does not determine admissibility, authority, execution permission, consequence, or final governance action.

---

## Is the observation sufficient?

The observation is sufficient to identify that runtime conditions have materially changed.

It is not sufficient to determine the correct interpretation of those changes.

Additional review, contextual evidence, and governance evaluation are required before downstream execution.

---

## What admissibility posture results?

Admissibility posture: REVIEW REQUIRED.

The observed semantic instability introduces uncertainty regarding represented reality.

Until additional evidence resolves the ambiguity, admissibility should remain provisional rather than assumed.

---

## What execution boundary results?

Execution boundary: escalation for governance review.

Execution should not automatically proceed based solely on this observation.

RiCo should require additional evidence, contextual validation, or human review before determining whether execution remains justified under present runtime conditions.

---

## What governance receipt would be produced?

A governance receipt should record:

- observation ID: nm-rico-syn-002
- measurement status: complete
- reduced stability and coherence scores
- elevated semantic instability
- high semantic drift
- suspected continuity discontinuity
- known measurement limitations
- authority not established by observation
- admissibility posture: REVIEW REQUIRED
- execution boundary: governance review before downstream execution

---

## RiCo Interpretation

This case demonstrates that observation can detect meaningful runtime change without determining the correct interpretation of that change.

Semantic drift is evidence that represented reality may no longer align with previously admissible assumptions.

Observation identifies change.

Governance determines consequence.

RiCo therefore treats this observation as requiring review before execution continues across the Runtime Execution Boundary.
