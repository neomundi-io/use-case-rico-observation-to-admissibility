# NeoMundi–RiCo Minimal Field Mapping

**Version:** v1.0 RC1  
**Status:** Working Draft  
**Scope:** Synthetic interoperability pilot  
**Purpose:** Define the minimum information required to connect a NeoMundi runtime observation with a RiCo Admissibility Determination and Governance Receipt while preserving clear architectural responsibility boundaries.

---

# 1. Architectural Separation Principle

NeoMundi observes runtime execution and preserves observational evidence.

RiCo performs **Admissibility Determination** under the applicable **Authority, Evidence, Context, and Policy**.

The **Runtime Execution Boundary (REB)** enforces governance consequence.

No observational signal directly authorizes, denies, or enforces consequential execution.

---

# 2. Architectural Boundary

NeoMundi produces runtime observations and measurement signals.

RiCo performs **Admissibility Determination** immediately before consequential execution at the Runtime Execution Boundary (REB).

NeoMundi observations may support governance decisions but never determine admissibility or execution consequence.

---

- Runtime Integrity Control (RiCo)

- Admissibility Determination
- Governance Receipt
- Constitutional Replay
- Authority
- Evidence
- Context
- Policy
- Consequential Execution
- Operational Continuity
- Legitimacy Continuity

These terms are normative and should be used consistently across interoperability specifications.

---

# 4. Field Mapping

The existing NeoMundi and RiCo field mappings remain unchanged.

The terminology above supersedes earlier wording such as "admissibility evaluation" or "RiCo governance receipt."

Use **Admissibility Determination** and **Governance Receipt** consistently throughout implementation documentation.
